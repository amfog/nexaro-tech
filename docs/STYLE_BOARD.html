Style board for the Nexaro Tech website

Hero sections of 7 well known software sites, taken 14 Nov 2026 in Chrome. For our own reference only (their designs, not to copy). Open the links to see the animations live. Pick the options at the bottom.

LinearDark

https://linear.app
StyleNear-black, one heavy tight headline, small grey subline, the real product UI right under it. Almost no colour.
AnimationKnown for quiet fade-ups on scroll, UI panels that slide in, soft glows. Nothing bounces.
What we could takeOur ERP and Teams OS screens under the headline; less text, more product.

StripeLight

https://stripe.com
StyleLight page, bold indigo, a big colourful gradient shape, customer logos in a row, dense but tidy.
AnimationKnown for the moving gradient wave in the hero and a scrolling logo strip.
What we could takeA single moving gradient in our cyan to purple, on light or dark.

RaycastDark

https://www.raycast.com
StyleBlack, glossy light-streak art in the brand colour, glass pill navbar, centred headline.
AnimationKnown for animated light streaks behind the hero and smooth product demos.
What we could takeOur particles become one strong light shape in cyan and purple; glass pill navbar.

SupabaseDark

https://supabase.com
StyleDark grey, one green accent on the second line of the headline, feature cards in a grid (bento).
AnimationLight motion: hover glows on cards, small illustrations that animate.
What we could takeTwo-line headline with the accent on line two (we already do this), services as a bento grid.

FramerDark

https://www.framer.com
StylePure black, white text left-aligned, the real product canvas big below the fold.
AnimationKnown for heavy scroll animation: sections scale, pin and reveal as you scroll.
What we could takeLeft-aligned hero with a big live-looking ERP screen; scroll reveal of modules.

ResendDark

https://resend.com
StyleBlack, elegant serif headline, a single 3D object, very little else.
AnimationSlow light sweeps and a rotating 3D object.
What we could takeA serif for headlines would make Nexaro feel premium and different from other dev shops.

Odoo (ERP competitor)Light

https://www.odoo.com
StyleWhite, friendly handwritten headline with highlighter strokes, app icons in a grid, purple brand.
AnimationHighlighter strokes draw in, app icons pop in one by one.
What we could takeFor SME owners: clear "all your business on one platform" message and a module icon grid.

Pick one direction (tell me the letter)

A. Linear and Framer: dark product-first

Black or navy, one bold headline, our real ERP and Teams OS screens big under it, slow fade-ups and scroll reveals. Closest to what we have now, more premium.

B. Stripe: light with a moving gradient

White page, a moving cyan to purple gradient in the hero, logos and numbers in tidy rows. Feels like a serious company; good for selling ERPs to managers.

C. Raycast and Resend: dark with one hero artwork

Black, one striking animated light shape in our colours, glass navbar, maybe a serif headline. Most "wow", least corporate.

D. Odoo and Supabase: friendly SME

Light or dark grey, module icons in a grid, bento cards for services, highlighter strokes on key words. Easiest for non-technical owners.

My suggestion for selling ERPs to Egyptian and Gulf companies: B for the home page (trust), with A for the product pages (our real screens). Mixes well with the current cyan and purple.
